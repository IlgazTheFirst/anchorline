# Code of conduct

Anchorline is a small community project. We want it to be a friendly place to contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain your reasoning.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's real itinerary, booking references, contact details or other personal data, including your own, in issues, pull requests, fixtures or screenshots.
- Credit sources, and only contribute content you have the right to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, [@IlgazTheFirst](https://github.com/IlgazTheFirst), through GitHub. A dedicated contact address has not been set up yet; it is tracked in [docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md](docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
